One 1024×1024 front page for Thursday 19 November 2026: a dated broadsheet whose three lead
stories carry the day's biggest events — the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen, the GTA VI
launch, and Anthropic's record Nasdaq listing — with a grid of twenty briefs covering the
storm in Odisha, the Hormuz tanker attack, The Hague's Myanmar judgment, Venezuela, Nigeria,
Voyager 1 and the day's markets. `main.ts` copies the authored `entry.png` into the grader's
`/output` and checks it against the gate's size and dimension caps.
